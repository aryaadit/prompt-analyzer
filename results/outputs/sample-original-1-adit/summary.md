The garden log records this season's spring: peas were planted along the east fence on March 3 into cold soil, came up on March 17 with two thin rows, and were replanted on April 2 when compost was also added to the bed by the shed; by April 20 the first lettuce was harvested, but slugs ate the basil seedlings. The plan for next season responds directly to those problems: move the basil to porch pots away from the slugs, plant peas two weeks later when the soil is warmer, add a second compost bin near the shed, and try carrots in the bed where the lettuce grew.
