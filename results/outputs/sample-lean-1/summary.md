The garden log records this season's spring: peas were planted along the east fence on March 3 in cold soil, came up by March 17 with two thin rows that were replanted on April 2 (when compost was also added to the bed by the shed), and the first lettuce was harvested on April 20, the same day slugs destroyed the basil seedlings. The plan for next season responds directly to those problems: move the basil into porch pots away from the slugs, plant the peas two weeks later when the soil is warmer, add a second compost bin near the shed, and try carrots in the bed where the lettuce grew.
